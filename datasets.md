## Hubble catalogues of exoplanet spectra

<div class="figs">
  <img src="{{ '/img/eclipse_spectra.png' | relative_url }}" alt="Hubble WFC3 eclipse spectra of 25 hot Jupiters" loading="lazy">
  <img src="{{ '/img/transit_spectra.png' | relative_url }}" alt="Hubble WFC3 transmission spectra of 70 gaseous planets" loading="lazy">
</div>

In [Changeat et al. 2022](https://iopscience.iop.org/article/10.3847/1538-4365/ac5cc2) and [Edwards et al. 2023](https://iopscience.iop.org/article/10.3847/1538-4365/ac9f1a) we conducted the largest systematic surveys of exoplanet atmospheres with Hubble: 25 planets observed in eclipse and 70 in transit, reduced uniformly with the [Iraclis](https://github.com/ucl-exoplanets/Iraclis) pipeline (see also [Tsiaras et al. 2018](https://iopscience.iop.org/article/10.3847/1538-3881/aaaf75)). The spectra were interpreted with population retrievals using Alfnoor, revealing trends in the thermal structure and chemistry of hot exoplanet atmospheres.

## Ariel Data Challenge 2022 — Ariel Big Challenge database

<div class="figs">
  <img src="{{ '/img/ariel_pop.png' | relative_url }}" alt="Population of simulated Ariel observations" loading="lazy">
</div>

For the Ariel Data Challenge 2022 ([Yip et al. 2022](https://arxiv.org/abs/2206.14642)), supported by the NeurIPS conference, we produced 105,877 realistic Ariel observations at Tier-2 resolution, complemented by TauREx 3 retrievals using nested sampling. The problem set-up and the database are described in [Changeat & Yip 2023](https://academic.oup.com/rasti/article/2/1/45/6998590).
